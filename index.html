What we make

Everything we sell, we make ourselves.

We are a manufacturer. There is no trading desk and no relabelled stock, so the range stays focused and your questions go to the people who made the batch.

  • Regular range

    Catalogue products

    A focused range of specialty chemicals, made on a regular schedule to a fixed specification.

  • Made to order

    Custom batches

    Need a different grade, purity or pack size? Send us the specification and we will tell you plainly whether we can make it.

  • Small packs

    Trial quantities

    Start with a small order, test it in your own process, then move to repeat batches.

How it works

From enquiry to dispatch in five steps.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us the product, the grade and the quantity you need.

  2. 02

    Quote

    We confirm the specification, pack size, price and lead time in writing.

  3. 03

    Batch

    Your material is made as an identified batch with its own lot number.

  4. 04

    Analysis

    The batch is tested against the agreed specification before release.

  5. 05

    Dispatch

    It is packed, labelled and shipped with its documents.

Quality

Every batch arrives with its paperwork.

You should not have to take our word for what is in the pack. Each lot is tested before release and ships with the documents your QC and stores teams ask for.

Certificate of Analysis
Test results for your specific lot, set against the agreed specification.
Safety Data Sheet
Handling, storage and transport information for each product.
Lot traceability
A lot number on every label, linked back to the batch record.
